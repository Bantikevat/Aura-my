## 🎯 The Scientific Problem (Why Rote Memorization Fails)

Students often reread books or memorize definitions without understanding the underlying mechanics.
* **The Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve:** Research shows that the human brain forgets **70% of new information within 24 hours** unless reviewed at specific mathematical intervals.
* **The Illusion of Competence:** Reading a book feels easy, but when an exam or real-world problem appears, recall collapses.

---

## 🔬 The 3 Scientific Pillars of Feynman Vault

### 1. The Richard Feynman Technique (Explain to a 10-Year-Old)
Nobel Laureate Richard Feynman famously noted:
> *"If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough."*

Whenever you add a concept to the Vault, it prompts you for:
1. **Standard Definition:** The formal academic or textbook statement.
2. **The 10-Year-Old Explanation:** Stripping away complex jargon into crystal-clear everyday words.
3. **Everyday Analogy:** Where does this physical or mathematical law manifest in real life?
4. **First Principles Root Cause:** What is the fundamental, irreducible truth behind it?

### 2. Ebbinghaus Spaced Repetition Vault
Instead of cramming before exams, the system schedules reviews on:
* **Level 1 (Day 1):** 24 hours after learning (solidifies short-term memory).
* **Level 2 (Day 3):** Bridges memory into intermediate synapses.
* **Level 3 (Day 7):** Reinforces synaptic pathways.
* **Level 4 (Mastered 🎓):** Permanent long-term cognitive storage.

### 3. Active Recall Test Arena (Self-Quiz)
Testing your recall is **300% more effective** than passive rereading. 
In the Active Recall Arena, the prompt challenges your brain to explain the concept from memory before revealing the answer.

### 4. Deep Focus Pomodoro & 432 Hz Ambient Tone
* Built-in 25-minute focus session timer.
* Procedural 432 Hz Web Audio synthesizer to induce a calm, high-focus mental state.
